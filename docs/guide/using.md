# Using 555

## Where the music comes from

- **Search** finds songs, artists and albums. Song info, covers and charts come from Deezer; the sound comes from YouTube Music, matched to the same recording (title, artist, length, skipping live and sped-up versions). YouTube Music versions that aren't on Deezer (remixes, lives…) show up too.
- **Your own files:** add your music folders in **Library**. MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A/AAC, WAV and AIFF. Tags and covers are read; your files are never changed.

Playback starts while the song downloads, and seeking is instant.

## The song menu

Every song has a menu with **Play next**, **Add to queue**, **Add to liked songs** and **Add to playlist**. Open it with:

- the **⋯** button on a song,
- a right-click anywhere on a song (lists, search, cards, the player bar) on a computer,
- a long press on a card or on the mini player, on a phone.

## Queue

Songs you add with **Play next** or **Add to queue** play before the rest of the list. The queue button (in the player bar, or under the controls in Now playing) shows them: drag to reorder, or remove them.

## Now playing and lyrics

Click the song in the player bar to open **Now playing**. Lyrics are word-synced, karaoke-style: each word lights up as it's sung. Click a line to jump there.

If the lyrics are a bit early or late for a song, hover them and use the timing control to shift them; 555 remembers it for that song.

On a phone, the **Lyrics** button switches between the cover and the lyrics.

## Playlists, likes and history

- **Import** a public Spotify, Deezer or YouTube Music playlist or album link (**Settings → Library → Import**). No Spotify account needed.
- Playlists can mix local, Deezer and YouTube songs.
- **History** lists everything you've played, day by day; **Stats** shows your most played songs and artists.

## Sound

- **Equalizer:** the button in the player bar opens a curve you shape by dragging three points (bass, mids, treble). Double-click a point to reset it.
- **Effects:** reverb and speed (the pitch follows the speed, like a record), or a preset: Slowed + reverb, Sped up, Bass boost.
- **Output device:** pick your speakers, headphones, Bluetooth or AirPlay device from the player bar; the music moves there without stopping.
- **Visualizer:** soft lights behind Now playing that move with the music (**Settings → Sound**).

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Language | English or French. |
| Appearance | Cover colors or a fixed color, intensity, themes and custom CSS. See [Theming](/theming/). |
| Crossfade | The next song starts while this one ends (0 to 12 seconds). |
| Resume | Bring back the last song, the queue and the position on launch. |
| Discord | Show "Listening to" with the title, artist, cover and progress (computers). Nothing is sent when Discord is closed. |
| Updates | Check for a new version (macOS and Windows update by themselves). |

Features

All your music, one quiet window.

yato finds songs on Deezer, plays them through YouTube Music, and plays your own files too. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Starts in a blink

Playback starts as the song downloads, and seeking is instant. The next song, the top search result and the songs you hover are fetched ahead of time.

Lyrics, word by word

Karaoke-style lyrics: each word fills with light the moment it's sung. Click a line to jump there.

Every word lights up on timeas the song moves on

Your files, untouched

MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, WAV, AIFF. Scanned on every core, searched instantly. Originals are never changed.

FLACMP3OGGM4AWAV

Bring your playlists

Paste a public Spotify, Deezer or YouTube Music link. No Spotify account needed.

Likes, playlists, history

Mix local, Deezer and YouTube songs in one playlist. See everything you've played, day by day.

Colors from the cover

The whole app floats in a mist tinted by the current cover. Frosted glass, fine type, soft blur transitions.

Discord status

“Listening to” with the title, artist, cover and progress. Nothing is sent when Discord is closed.

Keeps itself fresh

Signed automatic updates, and a verified yt-dlp kept up to date in the background.

FAQ

Questions

Does yato cost anything?

No. yato is free and open source (GPL-3.0), with no subscription and no paid tier.

Do I need an account?

No. Install it and press play. yato never asks you to sign in and doesn't collect usage data.

Where does the music come from?

Song info, covers and charts come from Deezer's public API. The sound itself comes from YouTube Music, matched to the same recording (title, artist, length, skipping live and sped-up versions). Your own files play straight from disk.

Can it play my own files?

Yes: MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A/AAC, WAV and AIFF. yato reads tags and cover art and never touches the originals.

How do I import a Spotify playlist?

Paste the link of a public playlist (or album). yato finds each song on Deezer and creates the playlist, no Spotify account needed.

Which languages does it speak?

English and French for now. Change it in Settings → General.

Is it safe? The app isn't signed.

Signing with Apple and Microsoft costs money each year, so the installers aren't signed yet. The code is public and the builds are made by GitHub Actions from it. Updates are signed, and yato refuses any update whose signature doesn't match.

Install 555 on iPhone and iPad

555 isn't on the App Store, so you install it yourself with SideStore, a free app that signs apps with your own Apple Account. The first setup needs a computer once and takes about 15 minutes. After that, everything happens on your phone.

Part 1 · onceInstall SideStore with a computer and a cable.
Part 2 · on the deviceOpen the 555 file in SideStore. Done.
Every 7 daysSideStore renews 555 by itself. Nothing to reinstall.

What you need

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 15 or later, with a passcode set.
  • A computer, just once: Mac, Windows or Linux.
  • A USB cable that carries data (many cables only charge). If the device shows up in Finder or iTunes when plugged in, the cable is fine.
  • A free Apple Account. It doesn't have to be the one on the phone.
  • Wi-Fi on the phone (mobile data isn't enough for the setup).
Tip: create a separate Apple Account just for this (free, at account.apple.com). You'll type it into SideStore's tools, which aren't made by Apple, so it's better not to use your main account. It only signs apps: it gets no access to your iCloud or your data.
Part 1

Install SideStore (once)

  1. Get iloader on the computer COMPUTER

    Download iloader from iloader.app (macOS, Windows, Linux) and install it. It's SideStore's official installer.

  2. Get LocalDevVPN on the phone PHONE

    Install LocalDevVPN from the App Store, open it, tap Connect and allow the VPN configuration.

    It's not a real VPN: your traffic doesn't go anywhere. It lets SideStore talk to your phone without a computer. It must be on whenever SideStore installs or renews apps.

  3. Plug the phone into the computer BOTH

    Use the USB cable, unlock the phone, and tap Trust when it asks, then type your passcode. On a recent Mac, also click Allow if it asks to let the accessory connect.

    Not showing up? It's almost always the cable: try another one, plugged straight into the computer.

  4. Install SideStore with iloader COMPUTER

    Open iloader, sign in with your Apple Account (the password is case-sensitive), select your phone, and click Install SideStore (Stable). Wait until it says it's done.

  5. Trust your Apple Account PHONE

    On the phone, open Settings and tap your Apple Account under “Developer App”, then Trust. On iOS 18 and later the button is Allow & Restart: tap it and type your passcode.

  6. Turn on Developer Mode PHONE · iOS 16+

    Scroll to the very bottom, turn on Developer Mode. The phone restarts; when it's back, tap Turn On and type your passcode. On iOS 15 there's no such setting: skip this step.

  7. Finish SideStore's setup PHONE

    Make sure LocalDevVPN is connected. Open SideStore and sign in with the same Apple Account as in iloader.

    Then go to My Apps and tap the 7 DAYS button next to SideStore. If it asks to create or revoke a certificate, say yes. Don't install anything before this first refresh.

That's it for the computer. You can unplug the cable: from now on, everything happens on the phone.
Part 2

Install 555

  1. Get the 555 file PHONE

    The iPhone version is a private beta for now: you get the 555 file (.ipa) directly from us. Save it to the Files app on the phone (from AirDrop, Telegram, Mail…: open it, then Share → Save to Files).

  2. Open it in SideStore PHONE

    With LocalDevVPN connected, open SideStore, go to My Apps, tap + at the top left and pick the 555 file. After a few seconds, 555 is on your home screen.

Keeping 555 working

With a free Apple Account, Apple makes installed apps expire after 7 days. SideStore renews them by itself in the background, as long as LocalDevVPN is connected at that moment. You can also renew by hand: My Apps → tap 7 DAYS next to 555.

If 555 does expire, it just won't open: your library and playlists are still there. Connect LocalDevVPN, open SideStore and tap 7 DAYS next to 555.

Updating 555

When you get a new 555 file, save it to Files and open it in SideStore the same way (My Apps → +). It installs over the old one and keeps your data. Don't delete 555 first, or you'll lose your library.

Something's wrong?

iloader doesn't see my phone

Try another cable (many only charge), plug it straight into the computer, unlock the phone and tap Trust. If the phone shows up in Finder (Mac) or iTunes (Windows), iloader will see it too.

SideStore says “Anisette” or can't sign in

It's usually SideStore's login server being down for a moment: try again later. Check the password too, it's case-sensitive.

It stopped working after an iOS update

An iOS update can reset the pairing between SideStore and the phone. Plug the phone into the computer, open iloader, click “Manage Pairing File”, then “Place” next to SideStore.

“Maximum number of apps” error

A free Apple Account can only have 3 sideloaded apps at a time, SideStore included, and 10 different apps per week. Remove another sideloaded app from My Apps.

“Untrusted Developer” when opening 555

Go back to Settings › General › VPN & Device Management and trust your Apple Account (step 5).

Is it safe?

SideStore signs 555 with your own Apple Account, on your phone. The 555 file is built by GitHub from the public source code. Neither SideStore nor 555 gets access to your iCloud.

Beta: the iPhone version is brand new and still being tested. Controls on the lock screen aren't there yet. Found a bug? Tell us on GitHub.
